# Nimbus Networks — Help Centre

## How do I talk to a person?
Ask for an agent in this chat at any time, or call the support number printed on every invoice,
08:00 to 22:00 IST every day. Email support@nimbus.example is answered within 24 hours. Business
plans get the support response times listed in the product catalog. When the assistant cannot
confirm an answer against our documentation, it passes the question to the support team itself.

## How do I raise a complaint, and what if it is not resolved?
Every contact gets a ticket number in the form TKT-12345; keep it for follow-ups. If a complaint is
not resolved within 7 days, write to the Grievance Officer at grievance@nimbus.example with the
ticket number. The Grievance Officer replies within 3 working days.

## How do I dispute a charge on my bill?
Dispute it within 30 days of the invoice date: Account → Billing → Invoices → Dispute a charge, or
contact support with the invoice number. Pay the undisputed part by the due date. While the dispute
is under review no late fee is applied to the disputed amount, and you get a decision within 7
working days. If the dispute is upheld, the amount is credited on your next invoice.

## Where can I see or download my invoices?
Account → Billing → Invoices lists every invoice with its PDF. Invoices are generated on the 1st of
each month; the Billing FAQ explains how to get a GST invoice.

## How do I sign up for a plan?
Enter your address on the Nimbus website or app to check that we serve it, choose a plan from the
product catalog, and book installation. Activation is typically 48-72 hours after the serviceability
check.

## Can I get compensation for an outage?
Yes. Compensation for downtime is an automatic service credit: if measured uptime in a calendar
month falls below your plan's SLA, a credit is applied to the next invoice without a ticket. The
credit table is in the SLA service credits section of the product catalog. Announced scheduled
maintenance does not count as downtime.

## How do I check my data usage?
Account → Usage shows your usage by day, updated every hour. On Lite and Plus you get an email and
SMS alert at 80% and 100% of your data cap, and you can add an extra alert at 50%. Past the cap,
speed drops to 10 Mbps until the cycle resets; we never charge for extra data on residential plans.
Max and all Business plans have no data cap.

## Can I schedule automatic payments (auto-pay)?
Yes, payments can be scheduled. Account → Billing → Auto-pay lets you pay by an eNACH mandate on
your bank account or by a saved card. The amount is debited on the due date, 14 days after the
invoice is generated, and you are notified 24 hours before each debit. You can switch auto-pay off
at any time before a debit.

## How do I change my plan (upgrade or downgrade)?
To change plan, go to Account → Plan → Change Plan. An upgrade takes effect within 2 hours and the
difference is pro-rated on your next invoice. A downgrade takes effect from the next billing cycle,
and you can downgrade once per billing cycle. Moving to Max needs the RX-900 router; a technician
swaps it at no charge. Annual plans can upgrade at any time and downgrade only at renewal.

## Is there a cancellation fee?
Monthly plans have no cancellation fee, early exit fee or early termination charge, because there is
no contract lock-in: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and partial
months are not refunded. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata minus a 10% administrative charge. A
leased router must be returned within 21 days of cancellation, or its price is charged (₹3,500 for
the RX-500, ₹6,900 for the RX-900).

## I am moving house. Can I take my connection with me?
Yes, if we serve the new address. Request it at least 7 days before you move: Account → Plan → Shift
Connection. We check serviceability at the new address, and a technician moves your service on the
date you choose. Shifting costs ₹500 on residential plans and is free on Business plans. If we
cannot serve the new address, you can cancel without the annual plan's administrative charge.

## How do I switch to another internet provider?
To leave Nimbus for another provider, cancel with us; broadband has no number porting: see "Is there
a cancellation fee?" above. Return a leased router within 21 days, and note that a static IP address
ends with the plan. Switching to Nimbus from another provider needs nothing from your old provider:
sign up as above and cancel the old service once we are installed.

## How do I report an outage or a fault?
Check Account → Service Status first; it lists known outages in your area and when they are expected
to end. If nothing is listed, restart the router and check its lights against the router manual. If
the fault continues, report it in this chat or by phone and you get a ticket number. Residential
connectivity faults are fixed within 24 hours on average.

## Do you offer mobile, SIM, roaming or TV services?
No. Nimbus Networks provides fixed broadband for homes and businesses only. We do not sell mobile
plans, SIM cards or phone lines, so there is no phone or SIM to activate or deactivate, and we have
no roaming, call forwarding, call waiting, voicemail or TV service.
