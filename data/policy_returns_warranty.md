# Nimbus Networks — Returns, Warranty & RMA Policy
Document ID: POL-RW-004 · Effective: 2026-01-01 · Owner: Customer Operations

## 1. Scope
This policy covers hardware sold or leased by Nimbus Networks: routers, ONTs, mesh nodes and
range extenders. It does not cover third-party hardware purchased elsewhere.

## 2. Return window
- **Purchased hardware:** 30 days from delivery, for any reason, provided the unit is in
  resalable condition with all accessories. Refund issued to the original payment method within
  7-10 business days of the unit being received at our warehouse.
- **Leased hardware:** may be returned at any time. The monthly rental stops on the date the
  unit is scanned in at the warehouse, not the date you ship it.
- **Opened but faulty:** covered under warranty (section 3), not the return window.

Return shipping is free (prepaid label emailed on request) for faulty units. For change-of-mind
returns the customer bears return shipping, or ₹250 is deducted from the refund if our label
is used.

## 3. Warranty
| Item                    | Warranty period | Covers                                  |
|-------------------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------------|
| RX-500 / RX-900 router  | 24 months       | Manufacturing defects, DOA, power supply |
| Mesh node MX-2          | 24 months       | Manufacturing defects                    |
| ONT / fibre terminal    | Lifetime of service (leased) | All faults except physical damage |
| Power adapters, cables  | 6 months        | Failure under normal use                 |

Warranty starts from the **date of delivery**, not the date of activation.

### Not covered
Physical damage, liquid ingress, power surge damage without a surge protector installed,
unauthorised firmware, removed/defaced serial labels, and damage caused by flashing the wrong
firmware branch (see router manual section 4).

## 4. RMA process
1. Customer raises a ticket or uses Account → Hardware → Report Fault.
2. Support performs mandatory triage: LED check, soft reset, cable reseat. **Do not raise an RMA
   without completing triage** — untriaged RMAs are rejected at the warehouse.
3. If triage fails, an RMA number is issued and is valid for 21 days.
4. Advance replacement is available on Business and Enterprise plans: replacement ships before
   the faulty unit is returned. A hold equal to the hardware value is placed on the card and
   released on receipt of the faulty unit.
5. Residential plans use return-first replacement. Typical turnaround is 5-7 business days.

## 5. Dead on arrival (DOA)
A unit that fails within 7 days of delivery is treated as DOA: immediate advance replacement on
all plan tiers, free shipping both ways, and no triage requirement beyond confirming the POWER
LED is red or the unit does not power on.

## 6. Escalation
If an RMA exceeds 14 business days without resolution the customer is entitled to a full refund
of the hardware price, or one month of free service if the hardware was leased. Agents may apply
this without manager approval.
