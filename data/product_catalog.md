## SLA service credits
Credit is a percentage of that month's plan fee, applied automatically to the next invoice.

| Monthly uptime achieved | Credit |
|-------------------------|--------|
| Below SLA but ≥ 99.0%   | 10%    |
| 95.0% – 98.99%          | 25%    |
| 90.0% – 94.99%          | 50%    |
| Below 90.0%             | 100%   |

Scheduled maintenance (announced ≥ 72 hours in advance, max 4 hours/month, between 01:00-05:00)
does not count against uptime.
